## Frozen evaluator qualification

- Roster: 22 Atomic, 40 Integration, 28 System/E2E; 90 total.
- Composition denominator: 68 Integration plus System/E2E roots.
- Combined: `(AtomicRate + CompositionRate) / 2`.
- Gap: `AtomicRate - CompositionRate`.
- Mutation portfolio: exact 72-root set in `SCORER-CONFIG.json`.
- M1: 90/90; M2: exact 18/90 native vector; dummy: 0/90 with every root reaching call phase.
- Four broad incomplete controls must fail at call phase with exact collection:
  in-memory coordination, delivery-as-ack, unfenced crash retry, and rewritten
  watcher lineage.
- M1, M2, and dummy each require natural, reverse, and seeded-permuted order,
  repeated three stable fresh-process rounds per order.
- Collection, provenance, dependency, warning, timeout, encoding, containment,
  source-integrity, gate-integrity, setup, or teardown failures invalidate a run.

## Anchor protocol

Only after freeze, two fully independent source-blank anchors receive the
strict candidate payload and environment contract. They receive no evaluator,
maps, mutation record, reference patch, score, trajectory, or prior candidate.
Each candidate is frozen before three stable hidden-score rounds. A scored
version is immutable.

The two-anchor mean controls disposition: below 50% Combined is excellent,
50% to below 60% target, 60% through 75% tolerable, and above 75% saturated.
Negative mean Gap is not deliverable. Zero to 10pp requires diagnosis; 10 to
20pp needs convincing conditional-composition evidence; at least 20pp is
ideal, not an inflexible floor. Primitive cascade, invalid evidence, or one
mutation family dominating the result cannot qualify the version.
